# Overlord Reigns: lore answers, L7 side stories (continues overlord-reigns-lore-answers.md)

Cleaned up after the final gap audit: every line here agrees with `overlord-reigns-canon-resolutions.md` (which still wins in case of doubt).

- **The Lich (Graveyard line, presented by Mortis)**: once a champion of Heaven's Peak's Fallen Knights, he turned to necromancy during the Silence and declared himself lord of the dead in the Overlord's absence, a would-be Overlord without the Netherworld's blessing. The graveyards are his domain; the **Bone Staff** is his emblem of command over them. Gnarl mocks him as an amateur, a "bone-collector with pretensions". He is UNRELATED to the Twilight Forest's Lich.
- **The Bumblezone**: a pocket realm of bees reached through a bee nest, sickeningly sweet, sticky and cheerful. A leftover of the old light magic the Empire failed to cleanse. Gnarl hates every golden inch of it.
- **Ratlantis**: a lost rat-civilisation dimension of cheese-powered technology; the Rat Baron is its flying ace. Rats are a useful workforce, but Ratlantis is where Gnarl draws the line on silliness. Played straight as comedy.
- **The Queen of Orchid**: a mantis-like guardian of a shrine to Evernight's REBORN elven magic. Another bloom of "elven rot", dangerous and beautiful. Its heart placed on the altar calls her out.
- **The Lost Castle**: seat of a noble court of the Glorious Empire. When the Empire fell, the magic-hating court hid it with a concealment enchantment anyway (the Empire was always a hypocrite about magic: its Sentinels ran on it), so it's "off the map". 300 years later the court lingers on as undead / pillager-like squatters (the Lost Court); its treasury is "What the Castle Kept". Gnarl adores the hypocrisy.
- **The Twilight Forest**: the refuge of **Queen Fay's Sanctuary elves** and the light-magic creatures that fled the Empire's "cleansing". A fae realm kept by elven magic, since opened to chaos, which is why its bosses run wild; its Lich is a fae-realm guardian twisted by that chaos (unrelated to the Graveyard Lich). The elves themselves are gone or hidden. It is NOT the source of Evernight's resurgence (Evernight regrew on its own).
- **Lestat**: has ALWAYS lived in the Overlord world (canon-merged, not a crossover). Ageless, but he NEVER interacted with or cared for Overlords until the fifth. Origin: Heaven's Peak's SILENT ORDER turned to worshipping the THIRD OVERLORD; Lestat's priestly SPLINTER group BROKE AWAY because of that and kept worshipping their old god, the **Silent God** (and did NOT sew their lips). He does NOT offer the Dark Gift. His ancient blood let him FEEL the Overlord join the vampires' ranks (First Blood), and he's curious to see where it goes. Bored and lonely.
- **Nycto's vampires and hunters**: NO connection to Lestat.
- **Gnarl and Lestat**: Gnarl REMEMBERS the cult (the Silent Order's Overlord-worship and the splinter) and is suspicious of Lestat, who answers to his own hunger, not the Netherworld.
- **The Collector (his name: the Historian)**: has ALWAYS lived in the Overlord world (canon-merged). He is DREDGE'S GENTLEMAN: courteous, cold, well-dressed, a collector of "art and artifacts, treasures and truths", who outfits the Overlord for dredging and wants particular relics from old wrecks. His aims stay MYSTERIOUS, as in Dredge. He presents Fathoms and relics/antiquities.
- **The Aberrations**: Wound-touched sea life, fish and sea creatures twisted by the same corrupted lifeforce seeping into the water (that's why the line unlocks from A Tainted Essence). The Collector prizes them as specimens of the rot. The **Sunken Scrawls** and **messages in bottles** were written by sailors who fished those waters and went mad; pieced together, they tell how the rot spread. Rituals: the writer invents within this.
