# Overlord Reigns: Archivist's answers log

Answers collected from the Archivist in batches. The writer fills the Twine; triggers, objectives and rewards are decided in the Q&A. Cleaned up after the final gap audit; `overlord-reigns-canon-resolutions.md` still wins in case of doubt.

## Canon and setting
- Strict sequel to Overlord I, Raising Hell and Overlord II.
- The Overlords in order (canon/wiki): 1 = Lord Gromgard (Dark Legend); 2 = the OI predecessor who possessed the Wizard; 3 = the OI / Raising Hell Overlord; 4 = the OII Overlord; 5 = the player.
- The 4th (OII) Overlord ruled by Domination. His dominated lands flourished, then revolted and brought him down (Fellowship of Evil is not canon). Background only; it doesn't frame the player's choices.
- Set centuries after OII, in the canon land (aged), after a ~300-year Overlord-less age, the Silence. Spree has forgotten the Overlords or thinks they're a myth, and learns the hard way.
- The Glorious Empire is gone; ruins and superstitious fear of magic remain.
- Netherrock: the Netherworld stone that raised the tower and carries Gnarl's voice.
- The Minecraft Nether = the canon Netherworld.
- Gnarl = the same canon Gnarl.
- The fifth Overlord: male (canon forms of address), silent (canon), a blank slate with no backstory.
- Can be referenced: past mistresses (all long dead), canon villains and heroes (Solarius, Florian, Kahn, Melvin, Oberon, Sir William, Jewel), canon lore objects (Forgotten God, Tower Heart, Exodus, Minion Burrows / First Minion), and Gnarl's personal memories of the 3rd and 4th.

## Main threat
- Antagonist: a faceless corruption, the Wound.
- The Wound: the canon Great Cataclysm (Florian tried to take magic from the Tower Heart, which exploded; magical plague, fear of magic, and Solarius's rise followed) also opened a dimensional rift nobody knew of in OII's time; firing the Tower Heart in OII tore it fully open. Over the centuries it has started to affect the land. The End dimension = the Wound. In the postgame, "Wasteland" is just a grand word for the Wound/End, with no link to the old minion home.
- Essence = corrupted lifeforce.
- "A Single Wound" doesn't reveal the End. It plants the idea that the forces eating the world share one origin that needs investigating.
- The Anchor (Sever the Anchor) = the Ender Dragon. Its Egg = a seed of the Tower Heart (A Seed of the Heart).
- The five "We Need a Bigger Sword" gates are ALL required before "A Single Wound". Each is a Bosses'Rise fight, and each boss is touched by the Wound:
  - Frozen North = Skor the Yeti (link "Silence the North" to "A Single Wound")
  - Ruborian Sands = Sirok the Sand Worm
  - A Dead Dragon Flies = Ashlord the Infernal Dragon (raised from the dead)
  - A Familiar Weight Below = Helvar the Underworld Knight = THE THIRD OVERLORD (OI). He escaped the Infernal Abyss because of the Wound, the corruption of both has driven him mad, and he guards an empty throne.
  - The Sea = Nerakyss the Kraken
- The 8 L_Ender's Cataclysm dungeons = optional scars left by the Wound. "The Acropolips" is the hideout's in-world name (keep the spelling).
- One ending, simple, built from stat tags instead of per-faction epilogues (see resolutions).

## Towers and minions
- Two towers: the overworld Dark Tower (with all 16 restoration rooms) and the Netherworld Tower (horde and the dead, canon-style: Barracks, hives, Mortis's Graveyard, totem, throne).
- Gnarl is never seen because he's always in whichever tower the Overlord isn't; his voice comes through the netherrock.
- Start: the tower has already risen. "Our New Overlord" is Gnarl's greeting, then "A New Master" (Browns).
- Hives: dormant, not lost. Strict order Browns > Reds > Greens > Blues. Rooms and exploration are open. Gnarl presents the hive quests. "A Complete Horde" is a celebration only.
- Hive objectives: Browns (A New Master) = obtain the Master Staff and summon a Brown; Reds = obtain a blaze rod; Greens = have the Poison effect, then kill a witch; Blues = kill an Elder Guardian.
- Awake from the start: the Browns and Mortis. Gristle and Blister wake early when the room restorations open.
- Mods: MinionsRemastered is the framework, and overlord_minions (custom) gives its 4 slots the looks of the four tribes. In gameplay minions are a WORKFORCE only. In the LORE they still fight, with canon roles. Beasts and companions fight beside the Overlord. Ignore TOBIsMinionEnhancements. Lifeforce is lore only. Horde size grows with the Overlord's power.
- Named minions (canon minion-speak; Quaver rhymes, Mortis grave and ritual) present quests on their own subjects instead of Gnarl:
  - Giblet the Sixth (descended from Giblet): Forge, armory, DragonSteel
  - Gristle (NEW, Red cook): Kitchen, farming
  - Blister (NEW, Green): Alchemy Den and the Theurgy chain
  - Grubbison Jr (descended from Grubby): Waystones, digging, travel, mining
  - Quaver: music, the Band, titles
  - Mortis: minion death, graveyard, Pet Cemetery, the Graveyard/Lich line (not the hives)
  - Gnarl: everything else (full table in resolutions, "Presenters (final)")
- Guest voices: Lestat (vampire line), the Collector / "the Historian" (Fathoms line, relics).
- No Mistress.

## Writing rules
- Humour: canon Teen level.
- Never break the fourth wall (no Minecraft or mod names; items are treated as real things in the world).
- Prefer original text + canon catchphrases; reworking a canon line is allowed when it fits the moment perfectly.
- No length target for the campaign as a whole, but per field: POPUPS stay 1–2 sentences; every START LOG, COMPLETION LOG and Rambling LOG is a small paragraph of 70–130 words, at most 9 short sentences, with lore, texture and a joke, never a one-line summary. Exception: key story beats may run to about 200 words (the opening passage, A Single Wound, the End reveal, the Anchor's death, the Heart's seed, the ending). OBJECTIVES stay short and clear. The writer reports each log's word count in the batch SELF-CHECK.
- Use the FINAL in-game titles from `overlord-reigns-titles.md` for passage names.
