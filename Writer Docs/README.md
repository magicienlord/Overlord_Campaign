# Writer Docs: Overlord Reigns

Everything the writer needs to fill `../Overlord Reign - Structure Authoring.twee`. Read in this order:

1. `overlord-reigns-canon-resolutions.md`: the Archivist's rulings. They override everything else.
2. `overlord-canon-bible.md`: canon facts and the voice sheet.
3. `overlord-reigns-answers-log.md`: frame, cast, presentation system, writing rules, workflow.
4. Lore: `overlord-reigns-lore-answers.md`, `overlord-reigns-lore-factions.md`, `overlord-reigns-lore-sidestories.md`, `overlord-reigns-lore-magic-cast.md`, `overlord-reigns-narrative-beats.md`.
5. Specs (triggers and objectives): `overlord-reigns-quest-specs.md` (part 1), `-part2.md`, `-part3.md`, `-part4-factions.md`.
6. `overlord-reigns-ramblings.md`: the 338 Ramblings, speakers and exclusions.
7. `overlord-reigns-titles.md`: final in-game titles and quest IDs (the specs use working titles; this is the lookup).
8. `overlord-reigns-writer-readiness-audit.md`: what was checked and what the Twine already contains.

**Canon lines to lean on heavily:** `../source/overlord_decoded/` (start with `gnarl_o1_lines.md` and `minions_and_titles.md`; its README maps the canon bible's citation codes to files). Voice models for the guests: `../source/Dredge/DREDGE_Collector_Only.md` (the Collector) and `../source/IWTV_Complete_Attributed_Transcripts/` (Lestat).

In the Twine, every quest passage has its template pre-filled with SPEAKER and CODER NOTES; the 338 Ramblings are unlinked passages tagged `rambling`.
