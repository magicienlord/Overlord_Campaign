# Overlord Reigns: lore answers (by inventory section)

Cleaned up after the final gap audit: every line here agrees with `overlord-reigns-canon-resolutions.md` (which still wins in case of doubt).

## L1. The world, centuries later
- **Place names**: canon names survive but the places have changed. Heaven's Peak has BECOME THE BASTILLE (the Pillager stronghold). Some places are forgotten ruins, e.g. the Empire's capital. EVERNIGHT has been REBORN in the absence of an Overlord, and Umvuthi (the Grove), the Ribbits, the Queen of Orchid and the Myrmex are direct results of that changed world.
- **Evernight's resurgence**: canon's "the Forest is starting to sing again". With no Overlord to crush it, Evernight's own elven magic regrew and ran wild. The Umvuthana worship a sun spirit (Umvuthi) that grew out of it; the Ribbits are frogs it awakened; the Orchid shrine and the Myrmex colonies are further blooms of it. Gnarl calls all of it "elven rot". The Twilight Forest (Fay's Sanctuary elves) is a SEPARATE refuge, not the source.
- **Heaven's Peak > the Bastille**: its own people (plague survivors / the city guard gone bad) became the Pillagers, raiders who fortified the city into the Bastille.
- **Power**: fragmented. No overlord of any kind since the 4th fell; the land is split among the factions, each ruling its own patch.
- **Religion**: Solarius's faith died with the Empire; only superstition against magic remains. The Church of Sin mod is NOT part of the lore. The Mother Goddess still answers Sacred rituals (Eidolon); the evil power behind Wicked rituals is unknown and nameless.
- **The Dark Tower biome**: cursed ground, blighted land that spread from the netherrock as the tower rose. Locals give it a wide berth. Leaving it completes A Reign Unfolds.
- **Dragons are back**: see L5 (Ashlord).
- **"The Silence"**: CONFIRMED. The Overlord-less age after the 4th fell, about 300 years ("three hundred years, give or take a lunch"). Gnarl can compare it with the canon Exodus (after the 3rd vanished).

## L2. The Overlord's rise
- **The netherrock**: a black outcrop of Netherworld stone that breaks through the ground wherever the Netherworld chooses a master. It "called" to the fifth like a hunger or an itch in the blood; it is now the overworld tower's foundation and carries Gnarl's voice.
- **The two towers**: the Netherworld Tower is OII's, where Gnarl waited all along. The overworld Dark Tower is NEW, raised by the Netherworld through the netherrock for its fifth master, which is why it's a ruin of empty rooms to restore.
- **The Master's Staff**: the Overlord's sceptre of command. Minions answer whoever holds it with the Netherworld's blessing. It lay in the tower waiting for its next master.
- **The hives**: without an Overlord, the Reds, Greens and Blues sank into hibernation in the Netherworld Tower's hives. Each wakes only when the new master proves himself with a feat fit for that tribe (blaze rod; poison + witch; Elder Guardian). Awake from the start: the Browns (with Gnarl) and Mortis (the graveyard's keeper never sleeps). Gristle (Red) and Blister (Green) wake early, individually, when the room restorations open.
- **The centuries / Gnarl's Secret Project**: while waiting, Gnarl invented the BIOMANCY school. Watching the slug Solarius (the Devourer, the Emperor's end in OII) and how magic corrupts flesh became an obsession and a cure for his boredom. With no Overlord to test it on, how sad. This is what "Gnarl's Secret Project" reveals.

- **Piglins**: DESCENDANTS OF MINIONS AND... well, a pig. A rather shameful subject for Gnarl.

## L4. The Wound
- **Gnarl's knowledge**: he knows the Cataclysm happened and that magic went sour, but NOT that it opened a rift. He works out the Wound over the campaign alongside the player (Suspicions I–V; A Single Wound = first suspicion of one origin; The Wound Beyond the World = the End reveal).
- **Signs**: monsters now carry "essences" because their lifeforce has been corrupted. Not so corrupted that the Overlord can't still draw minion-summoning power from it, but enough to become a real problem if left unchecked. The same rot reaches the sea (the Aberrations).
- **The Great Chalice & Netherman**: TIED to the Wound. The Chalice (the Vessel) concentrates corrupted lifeforce into armour-grade power (its Goblets). Netherman, "the Architect of Chaos", is a creature built from that corruption, Wound-touched but not the Wound itself.
- **The road to the End**: strongholds are EMPIRE CONTAINMENT VAULTS. Solarius's cleansers found the rift after the Cataclysm and sealed it beneath hidden vaults; the End portal is their sealed door. Eyes of ender are crystallised soured magic that yearns back toward the rift. Gnarl points the Overlord at them after A Single Wound.
- **The Ender Dragon (the Anchor)**: BORN OF THE CATACLYSM. The released magic crystallised around the fallen Tower Heart at the heart of the rift; its life holds the rift open, so killing it closes the Wound for now. The **End crystals** are rift-crystals, shards of that crystallised magic feeding the Anchor. The **Dragon Egg** it leaves is a seed of the Tower Heart (see resolutions). Respawning the dragon reopens the Wound a little: folly (Gnarl appalled but amused). The rift's outer reaches still "thrive" afterwards (the postgame).
- **The End Cities**: built by the Shulkers, a magical race that fled INTO the rift after the Cataclysm to escape Solarius's purge. The Wound slowly twisted them. A grim echo of the Empire's cruelty that Gnarl finds deliciously ironic.
- **The 8 Cataclysm bosses (scars)**:
  - The Harbinger: a DWARF MACHINE, revived and given life by the corruption.
  - The Ancient Remnant: an old RUBORIAN DEITY (not the Forgotten God), revived the same way as Ashlord.
  - Maledictus: a GENERAL OF THE FOURTH OVERLORD, imprisoned after the 4th's fall (the Frosted Prison).
  - The Netherite Monstrosity: a NETHERWORLD CONTRAPTION, corrupted.
  - The Leviathan, Ignis, Scylla, the Ender Guardian: see `overlord-reigns-narrative-beats.md` (Cataclysm scars).

## L5. The five gate bosses
- **Skor** (yeti of the frozen north), **Sirok** (sandworm of the Ruborian Sands), **Nerakyss** (a kraken that pirates worship): native beasts the Wound's corrupted lifeforce has bloated into monsters.
- **Ashlord**: a long-dead dragon brought back DURING THE GREAT CATACLYSM, the Kindred's success. He secretly birthed all dragons anew, then released them during the Silence. THAT IS WHY DRAGONS ARE BACK in the world (the dragon line). "Heraldry of the Raised" refers to him.
- **Helvar, the 3rd Overlord (OI)**: he CONQUERED the Infernal Abyss (Raising Hell) and became its god and prisoner. The Wound cracked the Abyss and he clawed out, mad and corrupted by both. He guards an EMPTY THRONE in the Nether: the seat he believes is still his. "One Reign Must End" is the fifth putting him down; Gnarl is torn between grief and pride.
