# Overlord Reigns: narrative beats (closes the writer-readiness audit)

## Main plot
- **We Need a Bigger Sword:** after A Tainted Essence, Gnarl feels the essence "pull" through the NETHERROCK like a smell on the wind. Five places reek far stronger (the five lairs), and whatever sits in them has swollen on it. The Overlord must grow stronger first.
- **Gate middle steps:**
  - **What the Frost Made:** the frozen skeletons are people Skor froze and ate. The Wound's corrupted lifeforce in his lair raised their bones. Lesson: the essence animates the dead too.
  - **Blood Beneath the Armor:** the corpse in the nest is a Ruborian raider in plate armour whom Sirok swallowed whole; his blood inside the armour has turned to essence. Lesson: Sirok feeds on corrupted lifeforce and grows from it.
  - **Heraldry of the Raised:** the dragon skull bears the sigil of the **Kindred** (the Minions DS cult that tried to revive the Dragon-Kin). Ashlord is their success, raised by the Cataclysm's magic.
  - **The Door to an Old Reign:** the Underworld Arena key opens a mock throne room in the Nether where Helvar has rebuilt the 3rd's old Tower hall from memory. Gnarl recognises the layout and goes very quiet.
  - **Part of the Sea:** the pirates on the kraken ship are a cult that worships Nerakyss and feeds it essence-laden sacrifices; their captain has half turned into a sea-thing. Lesson: the essence changes the living.
- **Freed essences:** flavour only (no item). Gnarl reads its origin in each boss's essence.
- **Gnarl's Suspicions I–V (NEW entries, count-based):** they unlock on the 1st/2nd/3rd/4th/5th gate completed, whatever the order. Rambling-style format (trigger popup + log entry, no completion).
  - I: "lifeforce gone off, like milk".
  - II: it spreads and animates the dead.
  - III: it has been at work since the Cataclysm.
  - IV: it's strong enough to crack anything, even what should stay shut.
  - V: all five essences bleed from one direction; there must be a wound somewhere (leads into A Single Wound).
- **Postgame:**
  - Return to the Wasteland: with the Anchor dead the rift should close, but it doesn't.
  - A Thriving Wound: the outer End is still alive with corrupted growth; the Wound's heart has moved outward.
  - Cities at the Edge: the Shulker refugees' cities, twisted but proud, with echoes of the Empire's purge.
  - Far Edge of the Reign: the catacombs and End tower hold the rift's oldest scars. Gnarl declares them the new frontier of the Reign (the hook stays open).

## Cataclysm scars (the remaining four origins)
- **The Leviathan:** a deep-sea beast from the flooded reef where the 4th's wave struck, fattened on drowned essence.
- **Ignis:** an Empire legion knight burned alive in the Cataclysm and still burning.
- **Scylla:** an Everlight sorceress who drowned outside the gates, twisted by the Wound.
- **The Ender Guardian:** a sentinel the rift made to guard its edge (the Ruined Citadel).

## Side lines
- **The Corrupted Champion = the Lich himself:** once a champion of Heaven's Peak's Fallen Knights, he turned to necromancy during the Silence to make himself a lord of the dead. The **Bone Staff** is his emblem of command over the graveyards.
- **The Lost Court:** Empire nobles who hid in the castle when the Empire fell. Their magic-hating paranoia sealed them in, and 300 years later they're undead / pillager-like squatters. Break the Lost Court = kill whatever hostiles hold the castle now.
- **Radiance for the Vessel:** the Vessel = the Great Chalice; Radiance = essence refined past corruption into raw power, which calls Netherman, the Architect of Chaos, a creature made of what the Chalice concentrates. Gnarl: "bottling a storm".

## World
- **The Silence lasted ~300 years** ("three hundred years, give or take a lunch").
- **Everlight today:** it still exists behind its gates, a sealed island of surviving elves. MENTION ONLY; not reachable.
- **Good / evil mana:** the Archivist's source is Gnarl's OI line about the smoke rising from dead Halflings. The script's exact wording is [O1 D1_MAIN 412.10]: *"See the clouds that rose from those dead Halflings? That's Evil energy, it gathers in all us Evil creatures."* So the campaign's EVIL MANA = canon's Evil energy (the dark clouds rising from the evil dead, which the Overlord absorbs), and GOOD MANA = its light counterpart (canon light magic). Eidolon: Sacred channels good mana, Wicked channels evil mana.
