# Overlord Reigns: canon conflict resolutions (Archivist's rulings)

These rulings OVERRIDE any earlier answer in the other lore docs where they disagree. Numbers refer to `overlord-reigns-canon-conflicts.md`.

- **#1 Numbering (canon/wiki order):** 1st = Lord Gromgard (Dark Legend); 2nd = the OI predecessor who survived by possessing the Wizard; 3rd = the OI / Raising Hell Overlord; 4th = the OII Overlord; 5th = the player. The Black Baron and the "eaten by a giant weasel" masters are older and unnumbered.
  - **How Gnarl refers to them:** he favours canon phrasing ("your predecessor", "my old Masters", "the one who went into the Abyss") but can number them.
- **#2 The 4th's fall:** Fellowship of Evil is NOT canon (no Shining Justice, no Netherghuls). His dominated people, i.e. **OII's dominated lands (Nordberg, Everlight, the Empire's heartlands)**, flourished, grew strong, revolted and brought him down. Their descendants are scattered or forgotten; none of today's factions descend from them.
- **#3 Helvar (the 3rd), canon-accurate:** the 3rd conquered the Infernal Abyss (in the Ruborian Desert), taking it from the Forgotten God, who cursed it to be his eternal prison; he became its god and prisoner ("he may still find a way to escape", Gnarl). The Wound cracked the Abyss and he climbed out, mad and corrupted by both. The "empty throne" is the Overlord's throne he still believes is his, since handed to others ("we may have to find a new Master").
- **#5 Good/evil mana:** KEEP "good mana / evil mana" as a deliberate campaign coinage for Eidolon's Sacred/Wicked split (canon itself only has one mana pool, plus light magic vs Evil energy). See also the Eidolon ruling below.
- **#6 Solarius:** Gnarl may call him "the slug Solarius" or the Devourer (the slug-like ooze of soured magic). Both are fine.
- **#8/#9 Spree:** the REBUILT OI Spree. It survived the Cataclysm at the edge of the old lands and grew over the centuries into a prosperous town: still sheep, pumpkins, beer and the Happy Mule, still "the loyalty of potatoes". It remembers neither the 3rd nor the heroes and has NO link to the 4th.
- **#10 Mermorphs:** in the 4th's wave (OII: the burning tar pits made "the seas start rising" and a wave hit the Empire–Elf battle on the reef), the Everlight elves OUTSIDE the closed Gates of Everlight drowned and became the Mermorphs. Those behind the gates survived. The Mermorphs remember being elves, but not the Overlord or the cause.
- **#11 The Twilight Forest:** the refuge of QUEEN FAY'S SANCTUARY ELVES and the light-magic creatures that fled the Empire's "cleansing" (not Evernight's elves, whom the Dwarves had nearly wiped out in OI).
- **#14 Lestat's old god = the Silent God.** When the Order of the Silent God took the 3rd as its new god, Lestat's priestly splinter stayed with the original Silent God and refused to stitch their mouths. Gnarl remembers the cult.
- **#18 Two gaps:** the EXODUS (canon, after the 3rd vanished: the Minions fled to the Netherworld and lost their hives) and the SILENCE (after the 4th fell, ~300 years). Gnarl can compare them.
- **#19 Gnarl's channel:** THE NETHERROCK beneath the overworld tower carries his voice (he is always in whichever tower the Overlord isn't).
- **#20 The Ancient Remnant:** a separate old Ruborian deity, NOT the Forgotten God.
- **#21 Ashlord and the dragons:** tied to the DRAGON-KIN of Minions (DS). The Dragon-Kin were extinct and the Kindred cult tried to revive them. Ashlord is the dead dragon that the Cataclysm's released magic succeeded in raising; he secretly birthed the new dragons and released them during the Silence.
- **The Tower Heart and the Wound (confirmed):** the Great Cataclysm (Florian's theft, pre-OII) first cracked the rift, unnoticed. In OII, firing the Tower Heart at Empire City released its energy a second time and tore the rift fully open. The Heart fell through into what became the End, and the Ender Dragon (the Anchor) crystallised around it. At the finale Gnarl SENSES the Heart.
- **The Dragon Egg = a SEED OF THE TOWER HEART:** the Egg left after the Anchor's death is the fragment of the Heart the Anchor crystallised around. Most of the Heart is still lost in the rift. The Overlord can take the seed (finale quest "A Seed of the Heart" / in-game "A Piece of Home", optional for the credits, see specs part 2). The seed CALLS TO THE REST of the Heart, and that pull is what leads the postgame outward into the Wound. The sequel hook is now "recover the rest of the Heart".
- **The Arena:** it echoes canon's Dungeon (OI: "The Tower can recreate many of the enemies you've conquered") and Battle Rock (OII, run by Gash). It is run by **GASHLING**, Gash's descendant, and still presented by Gnarl.
- **The ending:** in the canon End_Scene style ("And so the… fell. Ending, as such powers often do…"), narrated by Gnarl and built from stat tags: each faction's fate (P/S/D), the Sacred or Wicked path, whether he turned vampire, how many side lines he finished, and **whether he held the Heart's seed** when he left the End. It closes on "Evil always finds a way". CODER NOTE: it REPLACES Minecraft's own End scene (the End Poem and credits) and plays at the same moment, when the player enters the exit portal after the dragon dies, whether or not he took the Egg.
- **The Collector's name is "the Historian".** The old Ramblings list's "Historian" entries are his. He presents Fathoms and also relics/antiquities (Ancient Codex, Illager relics, Artifacts).

## Final gap audit rulings (25 Sept 2026)
- **Awake minions.** The Browns never slept. **Mortis never slept either**: he keeps the Netherworld Tower's graveyard, and the dead don't hibernate, nor does their keeper. **Gristle (Red) and Blister (Green) are woken early by the room restorations**: all room quests unlock together when the throne room (Seat of the Overlord) is restored, and that wakes those two individually. Their tribes' rank and file still sleep until the hive quests (Restore the Reds / Greens / Blues).
- **Eidolon: who receives the offerings.** SACRED rituals reach the **Mother Goddess** (canon; she answers with good mana). WICKED rituals reach an **unknown evil power**: Overlord canon never names its god of evil, and it is NOT the Forgotten God (he belongs to some larger pantheon nobody knows). Keep that power nameless and unexplained. The approved titles "Gods Like Snacks" and "Gods Are Just Landlords" stand as Gnarl's sneers.
- **Evernight's resurgence = the forest reborn.** Canon's "the Forest is starting to sing again": with no Overlord to crush it, Evernight's own elven magic regrew and ran wild. Its consequences are **Umvuthi and the Umvuthana, the Ribbits, the Queen of Orchid, and the Myrmex**. The Twilight Forest is a SEPARATE refuge (Fay's Sanctuary elves), not the source. Gnarl calls the whole lot "elven rot".
- **The Myrmex (The Mound-Dwellers)** are a consequence of Evernight's resurgence: insect colonies swollen by the regrown forest magic.
- **Two Liches, unrelated.** The Twilight Forest's Lich is a fae-realm guardian twisted by the realm's chaos. The Graveyard Lich is the rival dark lord (a former Fallen Knight champion turned necromancer in the Silence). Gnarl can grumble about how common liches have become.
- **The Aberrations** (Fathoms line, unlocked by A Tainted Essence) are **Wound-touched sea life**: fish and sea creatures twisted by the same corrupted lifeforce, seeping into the water. The Collector prizes them as specimens of the rot. The **Sunken Scrawls** and **messages in bottles** were written by **sailors who fished those waters and went mad**; pieced together, they tell how the rot spread. The rituals' details are the writer's to invent within this.
- **The road to the End.** Strongholds are **Empire containment vaults**: Solarius's cleansers found the rift after the Cataclysm and sealed it beneath hidden vaults (the End portal is their sealed door). Eyes of ender are **crystallised soured magic that yearns back toward the rift**. Gnarl can point the Overlord at them after A Single Wound.
- **The Lost Castle.** Hidden by a concealment enchantment the magic-hating court used anyway: the Empire was always a hypocrite about magic (its Sentinels ran on it). Gnarl adores the hypocrisy.
- **End crystals** are rift-crystals, shards of the Cataclysm's crystallised magic that feed the Anchor. **Respawning the dragon** reopens the Wound a little: folly, and Gnarl is appalled but amused. No mechanical effect.
- **Presenters (final):** Theurgy chain = **Blister**; Eidolon chain = Gnarl; Spell Study chain = Gnarl; Biomancy chain = Gnarl; Graveyard/Lich line = **Mortis**; Bumblezone, Rats, Orchids, Lost Castle, Twilight Forest lines = Gnarl; Dragon line = Gnarl (Forge quests stay Giblet VI); factions, main plot, Scars, Suspicions and postgame = Gnarl. Everything else as in the answers log and narrative beats.
- **Where the writer authors them.** The 338 Ramblings are **unlinked Twine passages** tagged `rambling`, one per Rambling, using a short template: SPEAKER · EMOTION · POPUP · LOG · CODER NOTES. **Gnarl's Suspicions I–V** are Twine passages LINKED from the five gate-resolution quests (Silence the North, Empty the Desert, Undo the Resurrection, One Reign Must End, Cut the Ocean's Threat); they fire by count (1st…5th gate done), whatever the order.

- **Held Ramblings (speaker availability).** Ramblings by Gristle and Blister are held until *Sit, Sire. Brood.* is completed; the Collector's until *That Is Not a Fish* unlocks; Lestat's (all 14) until *Pale Suits You, Sire* unlocks, so a never-vampire playthrough never sees them. Anything earned before release fires as a catch-up: log entry + toast, no popup. Details in `overlord-reigns-ramblings.md`.
