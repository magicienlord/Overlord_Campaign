Compiled 25 Sept 2026 from a full read of the decoded game scripts in the repo (all 96 .8ld files: Overlord I, Raising Hell, Overlord II), plus the Overlord fandom wiki and Wikipedia for Dark Legend, Minions (DS) and Fellowship of Evil (FoE). Script refs are `[file scene.id]`. **W** = wiki only (fan-written; speculation is flagged). The decoded scripts drop some italic words, so a few quoted lines are cut short.

## 2. Magic (canon terms)
- **One mana pool** only, a gameplay resource (Mana Pits, Mana Objects, recharged at gates or the Tower Heart). **"Good mana" and "evil mana" appear nowhere in canon.**
- The canon model is ambient **magical energy** that takes its nature from its host:
  - **Light magic**: "Light magic occurs when the magical energy that's all around us is absorbed into nice creatures… saving things and tinkling and shining and baking lentil cookies!" [O2 Nordberg_Sanctuary 300.20].
  - **Evil energy**: gathers in evil beings; rises as clouds when heroes or wicked creatures die ("concentrated Evil… accumulates in those of an especially Evil disposition" [O1 MK 107]); the Overlord absorbs it. **Dark Crystals** = "pure evil energy that's become crystallized" [O2 SQ D.C1].
  - **Soured magic**: magic released with no host goes bad, becoming the Wasteland **ooze** and the **magical plague** [O2 Wastelands 210].
- **Alignment systems:** OI **Corruption**, with 7 corrupted titles (Dabbler in the Evil Arts → Most High Lord of Chaos) and 7 uncorrupted (Watchful Guardian → Supreme Lord of Honor), plus paired spells (Legion of Terror / Legion of Honor; Infernal Shield / Sanctuary). OII **Tyranny: Domination vs Destruction** (plus Neutral); spells change with it. Gnarl: "Enslavement or Death, domination or destruction, these are the great Overlording choices" [NT4 402.15].
- **Lifeforce**: colour-coded orbs harvested from living things (even sheep); "Lifeforce equals Minions". Hives turn it into Minions.
- **The Tower Heart**: collects magical energy and "grows in power with its Overlord". It powers the Tower, holds spells, sets the Minion cap and carries Gnarl's voice (OI). History: the Castle Heart (Dark Legend) → looted by the heroes and used by Halflings to grow pumpkins → recovered by the 3rd → sabotaged in the OI finale → **exploded when Florian tried to steal it (the Great Cataclysm)** → reassembled from shards by the 4th, recharged by draining Queen Fay → **fired at Empire City's anti-magic shield**; Solarius's shrines caught its energy. **Fate afterwards: unknown.**
- OII spells: Evil Presence (hold to kill, release to enslave), Halo, Minion spell, Possession; Spell Stones and Catalysts. OI schools: Fire, Shield, Minion, Domination.

## 3. Gods and faiths
- **The Mother Goddess**: "mother to all races" but favours the Elves [O1 D2 800]. Worshipped by Evernight's and Everlight's elves (Everlight priestesses force-feed themselves to look like her).
- **The Forgotten God**: the Mother Goddess's ex-husband, caught cheating and cursed so that anyone who remembers him forgets him. He ruled the Abysses and tormented the dead heroes to be remembered. Defeated by the 3rd; destruction not confirmed.
- **The Order of the Silent God (the Silent Order)**, Heaven's Peak: white priests with **mouths stitched shut**, who heal and use holy magic, and hid in their temple during the plague. After the 3rd beat them, "They appear to have accepted you as their new god" [O1 D3 804.60]; his title was "New God of the Mumblers". In the corrupted ending they still call on their "Silent God".
- **The Order of the Red Dawn**, Heaven's Peak: a red-robed pleasure cult of the **Succubus Queen**, based at the Halfway to Heaven Inn, where the **zombie plague** began. Sir William summoned her.
- The Empire's **Solarius** was a false divinity built on stolen magic. People swear "Thank the Gods". Gnarl disdains gods ("knowing most Gods!").
- **Wraiths** are "spirits of the Evil dead" (Abyss).

## 4. Places
- **Mellow Hills / Spree** (OI): a peasant village of sheep, pumpkins and beer, with the tavern **The Happy Mule** (innkeeper-mayor **Archie**, from Ruboria). It was starving, lordless (Lord Spree died) and bullied by Halflings. Its people took the 3rd for a "hero", then betrayed him to Kahn ("the loyalty of potatoes"). Farmer **Bob**. **Castle Spree** to the north.
- **Evernight** (OI): the elven forest, "once filled with Elven magic… harmoniously". Invaded by Goldo's Dwarves (war machine **Rollie**, "Evernight's destroyer"); corrupted by **Oberon**'s nightmares ("the Dreaming"). Elves near-extinct (ghosts; last female elves enslaved). Features: the Sacred Grove, the ruined elven city, the Mother Goddess Temple, the Viridian Caverns (Green hive), the Skull Tribe. It **could be reborn** depending on the 3rd's choices ("The Forest is starting to sing again" [D2 1500.90]).
- **Heaven's Peak** (OI): the largest human city, in swamp-laden lands. **Angelis Keep** citadel; Paladin governor **Sir William**. The zombie plague ended when the Succubus Queen died. Fate after the Cataclysm: unknown.
- **Golden Hills** (OI): the Dwarves, **King Goldo Golderson** (mother Goldie Golddigga; cousin Goldon Goldeater). "Their empire is built upon" beer. The Royal Halls are collapsing from over-mining; there is an Arcanium mine. Dwarves are **absent from OII; fate unknown**.
- **The Ruborian Desert** (OI): Jewel's thief domain with Raiders, Blaster Bugs and **Sandworms**. In Raising Hell it holds the **Infernal Abyss** (the Temple of the Forgotten God).
- **The Wastelands** (OII) = "the old lands", site of the old Dark Tower, destroyed by the **Great Cataclysm**: ooze, floating rocks, mutants, the magical plague. The Empire dumped "contaminated" exiles there. **The Last Sanctuary** lies beneath it.
- **Nordberg** (OII): a snowy northern town of seals, Yeti and wolves; harbour **Nordhaven** with tar pits and oil wells; the Midwinter's Eve feast. Governor Borius. The **Netherworld lies under Nordberg**.
- **Everlight** (OII): a tropical island, "the ancient retreat of the Elves". The Empire turned it into a resort for fat tourists, with the Eradicator Facility and the Governess. Elves serve as servants; hippie idiom ("man", "bad karma"). The reef has elf-eared **mermaids/sirens**.
- **Empire City** (OII): the capital of the **Glorious Empire** (no other name), with an anti-magic shield, the Arena, the Palace with its vat, and Solarius's healing shrines. Slavery everywhere.

## 5. History beats to respect
- **The heroes' fall:** Melvin (gluttony), Oberon (sloth), Sir William (lust), Goldo (greed), Kahn (wrath), Jewel (theft/envy), each corrupted by the predecessor-in-the-Wizard.
- **After the 3rd vanished** into the Infernal Abyss: the **Exodus**. The Minions left the Old Tower for the Netherworld; hives were lost (Reds lured by wisps, Greens to a jungle island, Blues lost without a master). Then **Florian** tried to steal the Tower Heart → it exploded → **the Great Cataclysm** and the magical plague → fear of magic → Halflings "drummed out of their homes" → **Florian became Emperor Solarius** (masked, Marius speaking for him; sentinels, Eradicators, "cleansing" of magical creatures, magic stored in a vat).
- **OII:** the 4th burned the Nordhaven tar pits to melt river ice and free a ship: **"The seas have started rising, global warming's right on track!"** [MT 200.36]. A wave hit the Empire–Elf battle at Everlight reef ("The wave came... it was so big"). **Everlight's elves survived** as a people. Solarius fused with his vat of magic, became **the Devourer** (a giant slug-like ooze of soured magic) and was drained; "a pile of sludge on the ground".
- **Rose** (the 3rd's mistress, the 4th's mother, later Marius's wife) speaks of **balance**: "There cannot be light without darkness… The cycle starts afresh."
- **OII end:** "many things… seek to bring down an Overlord… Some come from the darkest pits and some from the shiniest cities… Because evil always finds a way."

## 6. Races and factions (canon traits)
- **Halflings**: "evil little blighters", gluttons who enslaved Spree men and kept trolls. After the plague they were driven from their homes.
- **Elves**: "more magic than meat", telepathic, Mother Goddess worshippers. Evernight's were genocided by the Dwarves; Everlight's became Empire servants; Fay's **Sanctuary elves** / Soldiers of the Sanctuary protect magical creatures (hippie pacifists).
- **Dwarves**: "angry beards on legs", beer, fire and explosions, war machines, greed; the old elf–dwarf wars.
- **Queen Fay**: fairy queen of the Sanctuaries, the Mother Goddess's "mortal embodiment" **W**. She sacrificed the Last Sanctuary and became Dark Fay (mistress) or Ghost Fay (dead).
- **Gnomes**: weak magical pests with "pointy hats of DOOM"; also firelighters.
- **Minions** are called "goblins" by outsiders (the Red Dawn Inn bars "goblins"; a peasant's "your little goblin gang"). This supports the Goblin-cousins joke.
- **Dragons**: OI's Forge makes "armor that could stop a dragon's breath"; OII has a dragon enemy ("Sire did slay a winged one!"), a "Dragon" champion title, and the loading tip "There's nothing noble about a dragon. It's 10 tons of flaming muscle powered by a brain the size of a gooseberry." **Minions (DS):** the **Dragon-Kin are extinct**, and the Kindred cult tried to revive them **W**.
- **Unicorns**: "over-rated pit-ponies"; Evernight's became blood-cravers.
- **Succubi**: Gnarl "knew a Succubus once"; a petrified succubus sits in his rockery.

## 7. Cast voice sheet
- **Gnarl**: "Minion Master and devoted Servant of Darkness". Old; can't bear grass and birdsong, so he stays home and talks through the **Tower Heart** (OI) or the **Overlord's helmet** (OII: "I bet you thought it was just a hat!"). Addresses: Sire, Master, Lord, Dark One, Dark Lord, Great One. Oaths: "By my scales / claws / hairy black toes", "oh thank Evilness", "Gnaah". Tics: disgust at the idyllic ("Hideous is it not? Try not to inhale it"); gourmet cruelty ("roast them like suckling pigs"); "I wouldn't dream of telling you your job"; practical evil ("rarely suitable for establishing a long-term Evil plan"); fat jokes about the 3rd ("Overlard"); lechery punished by the mistresses; anti-elf relish ("nothing does my black heart more good than a retreating Elf! Especially if they're on fire!"); hates fluffiness ("My eyes! My eyes!"); memories of old masters. Signature line: **"Evil always finds a way."**
- **Minion-speak**: "Yes Masta", "For me!?", "Niceee!", "That's mine!", "Kill! Kill! Kill!", "Fire no hurt it!", "Magic immune!", "Nottin Here", "sheepies", "shinies", "Spidies ours". Colour resurrection lines: Browns "I live to bash!", Reds "Flame on!", Greens "I live to stab!", Blues "I will heal again!".
- **Mortis** (an ancient Blue with a scythe): "Death for life", "Don't fear the reaper", "Minions. We live. We serve. We die." **Quaver** (Brown bard and chef, glass eye): rhyming AABB quatrains, fears being "made into mush". **Giblet** (Forge Master OI/OII, "Been around almost as long as I have!"): third-person speech ("Giblet will build. Oh yes he will!", "Giblet throw nothing away"). **Grubby** (Brown Digger): tunnels up Netherworld Gates, "drawn to your evil presence". Other canon names: Gubbin, Mouldy, Gash (Battle Rock), Rasp, Dribble, Gloob.
- Canon Minion **name style**: Browns grotty-thuggish (Clot, Grump, Basher); Reds fire and cooking (Scorch, Toast, Flambé); Greens stench and blades (Stench, Shiv, Pustule); Blues damp (Puddle, Drip, Squelch). **Causes of death** are short, alliterative puns ("Death by pointy hat", "Hugged to death").
- The **Jester** (OI) announces titles; in Raising Hell he mutters "Overlord... Underlord soon!"

## 8. Canon hooks useful to Overlord Reigns
- The 3rd is alive in the Infernal Abyss (in the Ruborian Desert) and "may still find a way to escape". This directly supports Helvar.
- The Wastelands / old lands have been ruined since the Cataclysm; the Tower Heart's fate is unknown ("The Tower Heart?").
- The Dwarves' fate after OI is unknown; the Heaven's Peak's fate after the Cataclysm is unknown.
- Florian/Solarius ended as soured-magic sludge (the Devourer). Rising seas since OII. The Empire hunted light-magic creatures; Fay's Last Sanctuary was destroyed.
- Dragon-Kin are extinct in Minions (DS), with a cult that wanted them back.
- The Silent Order worships the 3rd Overlord; the Red Dawn / Succubus cult also exists.
