# Overlord Reigns: lore answers, L8 magic / L9 cast (continues overlord-reigns-lore-answers.md)

Cleaned up after the final gap audit: every line here agrees with `overlord-reigns-canon-resolutions.md` (which still wins in case of doubt).

- **Eidolon Sacred/Wicked**: the campaign coins GOOD and EVIL MANA (canon has one mana pool; good/evil magic is light magic vs Evil energy). EVIL MANA = canon's Evil energy (the dark clouds rising from the evil dead, [O1 D1_MAIN 412.10]); GOOD MANA = canon light magic. SACRED rituals reach the **Mother Goddess**, who answers with good mana. WICKED rituals reach an **unknown evil power**: canon never names its god of evil, and it is NOT the Forgotten God (he belongs to some larger, unknown pantheon). Keep it nameless.
- **Iron's Spells**: canon Overlord magic, flavoured. Gnarl loves Blood; he uses Holy only as a weapon turned against its owners.
- **Gnarl's view of each school**: Theurgy = respectable old alchemy, dry but useful (presented by Blister); Elixirum (Blister) = potions, especially poisons; Biomancy = his baby, proud and defensive; Eidolon = rituals and sacrifice (Gnarl sneers at the priests who think someone is listening, even when the Mother Goddess does answer); Spells = raw carnage ("Magic is clean carnage, Sire. No wear and tear on your weapons", a canon line).
- **Minion personalities (APPROVED)**:
  - **Gristle** (Red cook): hot-tempered perfectionist who burns everything "on purpose"; speaks with a chef's pride.
  - **Blister** (Green alchemist): sneaky, twitchy tester who drinks his own brews and is always slightly poisoned.
  - **Giblet the Sixth**: gruff, proud of the Giblet line, hammers first.
  - **Grubbison Jr**: eager, over-excited digger, desperate to live up to Grubby.
- **Who's awake at the start**: the Browns (Giblet VI, Grubbison Jr, Quaver) never slept; **Mortis** never slept (he keeps the Netherworld Tower's graveyard). **Gristle and Blister** are woken early when the room restorations open (Seat of the Overlord done); their tribes' rank and file still sleep until the hive quests.
